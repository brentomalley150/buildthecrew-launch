Support

For the BuildtheCrew app, operated by Build the Crew Inc.

We're a small team, and a real person reads every message. Email us and we'll reply within 2 business days.

Email support@buildthecrew.com

If something isn't working, it helps to tell us your phone (iPhone or Android), the app version (Settings in the app), and what you tapped just before it went wrong. A screenshot is even better.

Signing in

BuildtheCrew doesn't use passwords. Enter your email address and we'll email you a 6-digit sign-in code. It comes from invites@buildthecrew.com and works for 10 minutes.

  • No code? Check your spam or promotions folder, and make sure the email address is spelled right. You can ask for a new code after a minute.
  • Code expired? Ask for a new one from the sign-in screen.
  • Sign-in code or crew code? They're different. Your sign-in code is 6 digits. A crew invite code has letters in it and goes on the Join a Crew screen.

Joining a crew

Someone already in the crew can send you an invite by email, or share the crew's code with you. Sign in, tap Join a Crew, and enter the code. You'll be asked how you can help, so Kira knows what to suggest for you.

Pilot codes

If your employer or organization gave you a pilot code, tap Have a pilot code? on the first screen after you sign in, or enter it later in Settings. It's best to enter it before you create your crew.

Leaving a crew

Email us from the address you sign in with, and tell us which crew you'd like to leave. We'll remove you and confirm. Tasks you created stay with the crew, so the rest of the family keeps their history.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time. See Delete your account for how, and what is and isn't removed.

Kira, the helper in the app

Kira suggests who could take on a task and helps your crew keep track. It never makes medical decisions, and nothing happens until a person in your crew confirms it. BuildtheCrew isn't an emergency service: in an emergency, call 911.
