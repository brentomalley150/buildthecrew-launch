What you're testing

An AI-coordinated crew for caregiving

When someone you love is aging at home, the whole family wants to help — but it usually ends up in a group text that never stops, with one person carrying most of it.

BuildtheCrew turns that group of family and friends into a crew. Everyone says when they're free and what they're good at, and Kira — the friendly helper inside the app — hands each person a short, personal list so the work gets shared, nothing falls through, and no one is left in the dark.

It's early, and it's real. You'll be one of the first people to use it — and your feedback directly shapes what it becomes.

Who this is for

Anyone helping care for an aging parent, relative, or friend — whether you're the one doing most of it, or someone who pitches in from a distance. Many of you already know this life firsthand. That's exactly why your feedback matters.
