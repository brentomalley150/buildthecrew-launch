Delete your account

For the BuildtheCrew app · last updated August 2026

You can ask us to delete your BuildtheCrew account and the data associated with it at any time, for any reason. You don't have to give a reason.

How to request deletion

  1. Email hello@buildthecrew.com from the email address you use to sign in to BuildtheCrew.
  2. Put “Delete my account” in the subject line.
  3. We'll confirm we've received it within 2 business days, and complete the deletion within 30 days.
Email us to delete your account

We ask you to write from your sign-in address so we can be sure the request is really from you — we won't delete an account on someone else's say-so.

What may stay, and why

A crew is shared by several people, so a few things can outlive your account:

  • Tasks you created or completed stay with the crew, with your name removed. Deleting them would leave the rest of the family with holes in their care history.
  • The loved one's profile and care needs belong to the crew, not to you. If you're the only member, that crew and its profile are deleted along with your account.
  • Ordinary backups may hold a copy for up to 90 days before they age out. Nobody at BuildtheCrew uses them after your deletion is done.

Deleting some data without closing your account

You don't need to delete your whole account to remove specific things. In the app you can edit or clear your own help preferences at any time from the Crew tab, and a coordinator can edit or remove the loved one's details from Settings. If you'd like something else removed, email us at the address above and say what.
