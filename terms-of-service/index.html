What BuildtheCrew is, and isn't

BuildtheCrew helps families and friends coordinate care for someone they love: tasks, a shared calendar, crew chat, and suggestions from Kira about who could help with what.

BuildtheCrew is not a medical service and not an emergency service. It does not give medical advice, diagnose, or recommend treatment, and nobody monitors the app in real time. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number. For medical questions, talk to a doctor or other qualified professional.

Who can use it

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. The person being cared for may join their crew with their own sign-in when the crew invites them.

Your account

  • You sign in with a code we email to you, so keep your email account secure. You're responsible for what happens under your account.
  • Give us accurate information, and keep your email address up to date so you can sign in.
  • Tell us right away at support@buildthecrew.com if you think someone else has used your account.

Crews and the person you care for

  • The person who creates a crew is its coordinator. The coordinator can invite and remove people, change the crew's settings, and delete the crew.
  • Invite only people you trust. Everyone in a crew can see the crew's tasks, calendar and chat, except tasks marked private from the person being cared for.
  • When you add information about the person you care for, make sure you have their permission, or the authority to share it on their behalf.
  • What crew members agree to do for each other is between them. We don't check, supervise or guarantee that anyone does what they offered.

Kira, our AI assistant

  • Kira suggests things, such as who could take on a task or an event to add to the calendar. She never does anything on her own: a person always confirms first. Items she suggests carry a Kira label.
  • Kira can be wrong. Check a suggestion before you act on it.
  • Kira never gives medical advice. Don't rely on her for anything about health, medication or treatment.
  • To make suggestions, the app sends parts of your crew's information to our AI provider, as described in our Privacy Policy. You can turn Kira's suggestions off in Settings.

What you share

  • What you post in the app (tasks, messages, notes, photos) stays yours.
  • You give us permission to store, process and show it to your crew, only to run the app for you and your crew, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • You're responsible for what you post. Please don't put diagnoses, prescriptions or detailed health records in the app.
