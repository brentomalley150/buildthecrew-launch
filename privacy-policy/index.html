Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

BuildtheCrew is a mobile app that helps families and friends coordinate care for someone they love. It is operated by Build the Crew Inc. (“BuildtheCrew,” “we,” “us,” “our”). This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. It covers the BuildtheCrew mobile app on iOS and Android, and the waitlist and informational pages on buildthecrew.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information. Your email address, used to create your account and sign you in with a one-time code. BuildtheCrew does not use passwords.
  • Your name and how you’d like to be shown to your crew.
  • Crew and care details you enter. The crew’s name, the first name (or nickname) of the person being cared for, an optional approximate age and ZIP code, the kinds of help that person needs, and what each crew member can help with.
  • Content you create in the app. Tasks, calendar events, and messages you send in your crew’s chat.
  • Invitations. The email addresses you enter to invite people to your crew.
  • Waitlist and pricing responses you submit on our website, if you joined the waitlist.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device analytics. How you use the app — screens viewed, features used, and similar events — along with basic device information, to understand what’s working and improve the product.
  • Push notification token. A device token so we can send you notifications (for example, when a task is assigned or a message arrives). You can turn notifications off in your device settings.
We ask only for the basics needed to coordinate care. We do not ask for, and you should not enter, medical records, prescriptions, diagnoses, or detailed health information. See A note on health information.

How we use information

  • To provide the app: create your account, run your crew, and keep tasks, events, and messages in sync across everyone in the crew.
  • To send you a sign-in code and, if you’ve enabled them, push notifications about your crew.
  • To let Kira, our AI assistant, suggest how to share the work (see below).
  • To understand how the app is used so we can fix problems and improve it.
  • To communicate with you about the app, and — only if you’re open to it — to learn about your situation.
  • To keep the service safe and secure and to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

How Kira (our AI assistant) uses information

BuildtheCrew includes an AI assistant named Kira that suggests ways to share the work — for example, pointing out a kind of help no one has signed up for, suggesting tasks a member could pick up, or spotting an appointment mentioned in chat and offering to add it to the calendar.

To do this, the app sends the relevant parts of your crew’s information — which can include tasks, who is in the crew and what they can help with, the first name of the person being cared for, and, for the calendar feature, the text of recent crew chat messages — to our AI provider, Anthropic, which runs the Claude model that powers Kira. Anthropic processes this information to generate Kira’s suggestions and, under our agreement, does not use it to train its models.

  • Kira suggests; it never acts on its own. Nothing Kira proposes happens until a person in your crew approves it.
  • Kira does not give medical advice. It will not interpret, summarize, or advise on health information, even if a message mentions it.
  • Every suggestion Kira makes is labeled as coming from Kira and shows what crew information it was based on.

Sharing within your crew

BuildtheCrew is a shared, collaborative space. Information you add to a crew — your name, tasks, events, and chat messages — is visible to the other members of that crew. Invitations you send reveal that you have invited that person. Please share only what you’re comfortable with the rest of your crew seeing.

The person being cared for

If the person being cared for has their own account, they see a limited view: what the crew is planning for them. They do not see the crew’s private chat, and they do not see any task a crew member marks as private. If that person does not use the app, the crew can still record things on their behalf. We ask only for a first name or nickname and, optionally, an approximate age and ZIP code — never detailed records.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and your crew is using BuildtheCrew, and as needed to provide the service. When you delete your account (see below), we delete or de-identify your personal information, except where we need to keep certain records to comply with the law, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Chat messages are part of a shared crew record and may remain visible to the crew even after an individual message is retracted.

Your choices and rights

  • Notifications. Turn push notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • Access, correction, and deletion. You can edit much of your information in the app, and you can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information by contacting us.
  • Email. Our informational emails include a one-click unsubscribe. Sign-in codes and invitation emails are necessary to use the service.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA — including the right to access, correct, delete, or port your information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. We do not sell or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under California law. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Deleting your account

You can delete your BuildtheCrew account at any time. We provide an in-app option to delete your account, and you can also request deletion from our account deletion page or by emailing us. When you delete your account, we remove your personal information as described in “How long we keep information.”

How we protect information

We use reputable providers and reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information in transit and at rest, and the app enforces access rules so that only members of a crew can see that crew’s information. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we work to protect what you share.

Children

BuildtheCrew is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

A note on health information

BuildtheCrew helps coordinate care logistics — rides, meals, check-ins, errands, appointments — not medical care. The app is not a medical service and is not intended to store medical records or protected health information, and Kira does not provide medical advice. Please do not enter diagnoses, prescriptions, or detailed health information into the app.

Changes to this policy

As BuildtheCrew grows, we may update this policy. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app or by email. Your continued use of BuildtheCrew after an update means you accept the revised policy.
