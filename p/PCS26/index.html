Welcome to the BuildtheCrew pilot
for Principle Choice Solutions associates.

BuildtheCrew helps the family and friends caring for someone at home share the load. Getting started takes about three minutes: install the app, sign in, and enter your pilot code.

1

Install the app

Tap the button for your phone.

iPhoneGet it via TestFlight AndroidGet it on Google Play

iPhone: you'll first be asked to install Apple's free TestFlight app, then open the link again and tap Accept, then Install. The app appears as BTheCrew.
Android: tap Become a tester, then Download it on Google Play. If Play says the app isn't found, wait a few minutes and try again.

2

Sign in

Open BuildtheCrew and sign in with your email. We'll send you a short code to confirm it's you.

3

Enter your pilot code

Your pilot code
PCS26

In the app, tap Have a pilot code? and enter PCS26. You'll find it:

  • on the welcome screen right after you sign in (best: do this before you create your crew), or
  • any time later in Settings.

Then build your crew

Add the person you help care for and invite the family and friends who pitch in. Kira, the helper inside the app, suggests who can take what so no one carries it alone. Kira helps you coordinate; it never makes medical decisions.
