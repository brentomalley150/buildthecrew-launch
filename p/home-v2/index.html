The problem

The hardest part isn't the tasks. It's keeping track of them.

Someone has to notice what's coming up, remember who said what, and ask for help. That job usually lands on one person. Nobody planned it that way.

Privacy & trust

Kira suggests. Your family decides.

Caring for a parent is personal. Kira was built with firm limits, and she keeps to them.

Nothing happens without a tap

Kira never moves, assigns or adds anything on her own. Every suggestion waits for someone in the crew to say yes.

She shows her work

Every suggestion says what it's based on: which tasks, which message, what someone said they can do. If she isn't sure, she asks instead of guessing.

She stays out of medical

Kira helps with coordination: who's doing what, and when. She doesn't give medical advice, and she keeps health details out of her summaries and the calendar.

She reads the chat only when asked

Kira looks at what your crew wrote only when someone asks her to check the chat for plans. Your crew's information is never sold.

Questions families ask

Good questions

Who is Kira?

Kira is the AI helper built into BuildtheCrew. She looks at your crew's tasks, calendar and what each person said they can help with, and suggests what to do next. She's not a person, and she never acts on her own.

Is Kira reading our messages?

Only when someone asks her to check the chat for plans like appointments or rides. Then she shows you exactly which message she found. The rest of the time she doesn't read what you write.

Does Kira give medical advice?

No. Kira helps with coordination: who's doing what, and when. She doesn't give medical advice and keeps health details out of her summaries and the calendar. Always talk to your loved one's doctor about medical questions.

Can Kira change things without asking?

No. Kira suggests, and someone in your crew taps to say yes. She never moves a task, assigns someone, or adds to the calendar on her own.

Can friends and neighbors join, or only family?

Anyone who helps can join: family, friends, neighbors. Each person says how they can help, so Kira only suggests things that fit them.

My mom isn't great with phones. Can she still be part of it?

Yes. Your loved one can join with a simple view that shows what the crew is doing and lets them ask for something in one line. They don't have to join for the crew to work, though.

Do I need a password?

No. You sign in with your email and a code we send you.
